About PAWFREE

Rethinking waste for remote hospitality

The Problem

Remote Operations Deserve a Better Waste System

Safari camps and remote hospitality operations operate in some of the world's most environmentally sensitive landscapes.

Waste management therefore cannot simply be about removing rubbish. It needs to be about what happens to every material after it leaves the camp. Traditional disposable sacks create an additional waste stream. They can also make separated materials difficult to identify, increase handling requirements and ultimately add to the amount of waste requiring disposal.

PAWFREE addresses the problem at multiple points:

Single-Use Sacks

Repeatedly purchasing and disposing of conventional waste sacks creates unnecessary packaging waste.

Single-Use Sacks

Poor Visibility

When different waste streams are transported in similar sacks, identification becomes difficult.

Poor Visibility

Contamination

Poor separation can reduce the value and recyclability of recovered materials.

Contamination

Landfill Pressure

Recyclable materials that could have been recovered may instead end up in landfill.

Landfill Pressure

Remote Logistics

Moving waste from remote operations requires a system designed around efficiency, durability and repeated use.

Remote Logistics

Unnecessary Packaging

Fresh produce and other supplies can arrive at remote camps surrounded by disposable packaging that becomes waste almost immediately.

Unnecessary Packaging
Our Solution

A Complete Circular System — Not Just a Bag

PAWFREE is designed around one simple principle:

Keep products and materials in use for as long as possible.

Our system combines reusable waste bags, colour-coded separation, collection logistics, recycling connections and reusable supply packaging.

Instead of treating waste as something that simply needs to disappear, PAWFREE helps businesses create a more controlled journey for materials.

Colour-Coded Waste System

Colour-Coded for the Bush

Waste separation should be simple enough to understand at a glance. PAWFREE bags use a dedicated colour system to identify waste streams without opening the bag.

Bush-Sensitive Colours — our colour palette is deliberately selected to be sensitive to the natural bush environment. Rather than introducing bright, visually intrusive colours into safari landscapes, PAWFREE uses earth-inspired, muted and natural tones that complement the environments where safari companies operate.

Beige — Glass Bottles

A natural, understated tone that blends comfortably with earthy safari environments.

Brown — Plastic

An earth-inspired colour supporting easy identification while remaining visually discreet.

Grey — Aluminium / Cans

A neutral industrial tone that communicates metal without becoming visually dominant.

Black — Paper

A clean, professional neutral that provides strong identification and branding.

Visible enough for operational efficiency. Subtle enough for the wilderness.

The colour-coded system improves sorting accuracy, reduces contamination and helps collection and recycling teams identify materials quickly.

The Closed-Loop Service

Clean. Sorted. Returned. Reused.

PAWFREE is designed to keep reusable bags circulating.

01

Separate

Waste is separated at the point where it is generated.

02

Store

Each waste stream is placed into its dedicated PAWFREE bag.

03

Collect

PAWFREE collection logistics move the bags from remote operations through the waste management chain.

04

Empty

Waste is removed from the reusable bags.

05

Recycle

Recyclable materials are directed to PAWFREE's recycling partners.

06

Clean

Empty reusable bags are cleaned and sanitised.

07

Inspect

Bags are checked to ensure they remain safe and serviceable.

08

Return

Clean bags are returned for another cycle.

Repeat — the same reusable system continues working instead of becoming waste after one journey.

This closed-loop model builds on PAWFREE's existing service concept of collecting, cleaning, sterilising, inspecting and returning reusable bags.

From Waste to Resource

We Don't Just Move Waste. We Help Move Materials Toward Their Next Use.

A major part of PAWFREE's model is creating a stronger connection between waste generators and recycling markets. Rather than treating landfill as the automatic destination for collected materials, PAWFREE works to direct recyclable waste streams to appropriate recycling partners.

The PAWFREE Journey

Camp → PAWFREE Collection → Sorting → Recycling Partner → Recycling / Recovery

Instead Of

Camp → Disposable Sack → Collection → Landfill

  • Divert recyclable materials away from landfill
  • Reduce the volume of waste sent for disposal
  • Reduce pressure on landfill capacity
  • Improve recovery of valuable materials
  • Maintain cleaner, more consistent recyclable streams
  • Provide businesses with measurable waste-diversion data

Landfill is not the destination for everything. Where a material has a viable recycling or recovery pathway, PAWFREE aims to help keep it within the circular economy.

Reusable Fresh-Produce Bags

Stop Packaging Waste Before It Reaches the Camp

The most effective waste is waste that never needs to be created. Fresh vegetables and other food products can arrive at remote camps surrounded by disposable plastic bags, cardboard boxes and other packaging — and once the product reaches the camp, much of that packaging has one destination: the waste stream. PAWFREE provides reusable food and fresh-produce bags designed to replace this unnecessary packaging in the supply chain.

Reusable Journey

Supplier → Reusable PAWFREE Bag → Camp → Return → Reuse

Instead Of

Supplier → Disposable Packaging → Camp → Waste

Designed for:

Fresh Vegetables Fresh Produce Meat Dry Food Products Camp Supplies Other Hospitality Supplies

PAWFREE's original proposal already identifies reusable transport bags for fresh vegetables, meat, dry food and other camp supplies as a way of replacing plastic bags, cardboard and polystyrene packaging.
