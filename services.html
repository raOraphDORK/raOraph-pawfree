OUR SOLUTIONS

Waste systems built for remote hospitality

Explore Our Solutions

Every lodge and camp faces the same challenge: no municipal collection, high transport costs, and fragile ecosystems that can't absorb single-use waste. PAWFREE closes that gap with a full, end-to-end system — from reusable packaging on delivery, to on-site sorting, to transport that connects your waste stream directly to recycling partners.

Reusable Packaging

Durable, returnable packaging that replaces single-use plastic on every delivery run.

Waste Sorting Systems

On-site sorting stations and staff training so recyclables never end up landfilled.

Collection & Transport

Scheduled pickups that move sorted waste out of remote camps efficiently and reliably.

Recycling Partnerships

Direct connections to recycling partners, so your waste stream becomes someone else's supply.

Partner with PAWFREE

Whether you run a single lodge or a portfolio of camps, we'll design a waste system around your operation — not the other way around. Get in touch and we'll walk you through what it looks like for your site.
