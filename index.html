The Wild Should Never Have To Carry Our Waste

Remote safari operations face a unique challenge. Waste may be carefully separated at camp, but the journey after separation can still depend on disposable sacks, inefficient transportation and landfill disposal.

PAWFREE changes that.

We provide reusable, colour-coded waste bags, collection systems and reusable packaging solutions that keep materials separated, reduce single-use waste and create a direct pathway from remote operations to recycling.
