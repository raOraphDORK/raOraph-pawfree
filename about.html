Our Story

GRAS LERA GROUP, trading as PAWFREE, was founded to solve a problem unique to remote hospitality: waste has nowhere to go. Safari lodges and camps operate far from municipal collection routes, and single-use packaging piles up fast in some of the world's most sensitive ecosystems.

We built a circular waste and reusable packaging system specifically for safari camps, remote hospitality operations, and environmentally responsible businesses — helping them reduce single-use packaging, separate waste correctly, move recyclable materials efficiently, and connect waste streams directly with recycling partners.

Protect the Wild

Keeping waste out of sensitive ecosystems that safari operations depend on.

Circular by Design

Packaging and waste systems built to be reused, sorted, and recovered — not landfilled.

Community Partnership

Working directly with local collectors and recycling partners to create real, lasting jobs.
